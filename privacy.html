Privacy policy

This page explains how information is handled on Pranjal Saini's personal portfolio and its arcade games. Contact: pranjalsaini3030@gmail.com.

Browsing and playing

The site does not use advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, accounts or tracking cookies. The games run in your browser. Scores and game state are kept in memory for the current session and are not saved to browser storage or submitted to a server. Reloading the page clears them.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser details and request times, to deliver and protect its services. Its practices are described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Email and external links

Selecting an email link opens your email application. Sending a message shares the information you choose to include with Pranjal Saini and the email providers involved, so that your enquiry can be answered. Avoid including sensitive information that is not needed for your enquiry. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and Instagram take you to separate services with their own privacy policies.

Your requests

To ask about information you have sent by email, or to request correction or deletion, write to the contact address above. Applicable privacy rights depend on your circumstances and location. Hosting-provider records are managed by that provider.

Changes

This page will be updated if the site's data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.
